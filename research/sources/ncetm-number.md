 A secure understanding of number is the platform upon which all mathematical understanding is based. This page contains links to seven ‘question documents', each helping teachers to assess their confidence in a specific aspect of this area of maths.

 All question documents for this area are available as a single zip file download after the individual questions. At the bottom of this page you can find a series of short videos giving advice on how the topics might be handled in the classroom. 

 Question 1

 How confident are you that you understand and can support children to use and apply the principles of counting?

 Question 2

 How confident are you that you understand and can support children to use mathematical symbols to show relationships and compare quantities?

 Question 3

 How confident are you that you understand and can support children to identify small quantities through perceptual or conceptual subitising?

 Question 4

 How confident are you that you understand and can support children to compose and decompose numbers, using representations to support this?

 Question 5

 How confident are you that you understand and can support children to unitise?

 Question 6

 How confident are you that you understand and can support children to recognise the structure of the place value system, linking to concepts including decimals and negative numbers?

 Question 7

 How confident are you that you understand and can support children to estimate and round using their knowledge of the relative position of numbers?
