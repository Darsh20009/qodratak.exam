Add and Subtract Decimals Let’s take one more look at the lunch order from the start of Decimals , this time noticing how the numbers were added together. All three items (sandwich, water, tax) were priced in dollars and cents, so we lined up the dollars under the dollars and the cents under the cents, with the decimal point s lined up between them. Then we just added each column, as if we were adding whole numbers. By lining up decimal s this way, we can add or subtract the corresponding place values just as we did with whole numbers.
