Model Subtraction of Integers Remember the story in the last section about the toddler and the cookies? Children learn how to subtract numbers through their everyday experiences. Real-life experiences serve as models for subtracting positive numbers, and in some cases, such as temperature, for adding negative as well as positive numbers. But it is difficult to relate subtracting negative numbers to common life experiences. Most people do not have an intuitive understanding of subtraction when negative numbers are involved. Math teachers use several different models to explain subtracting negative numbers. We will continue to use counters to model subtraction. Remember, the blue counters represent positive numbers and the red counters represent negative numbers. Perhaps when you were younger, you read 5 − 3 5 − 3 as five take away three . When we use counters, we can think of subtraction the same way.
