### Saudi Qudrat scope

The official ETEC guidance and practice PDFs establish an appropriate Saudi exam context, and the official scientific practice sample includes a quantitative word problem about distributing dates into 1 kg and 2 kg packages. The sample supports teaching students to translate a verbal situation into quantities and an equation; its wording and numbers must not be reused in the book.

The public evidence collected here does **not** establish a complete current quantitative blueprint, topic weights, or the exact frequency of each skill. The proposed curriculum is therefore a school-mathematics foundation for aptitude reasoning, not a claim that ETEC publishes or endorses this exact 50-unit sequence.

### Quantitative topic sequence

OpenStax Prealgebra 2e provides a coherent prerequisite sequence: divisibility and factors, prime factorization and least common multiples, integer operations, fractions, decimals, proportions, then roots, geometry, statistics, probability, and slope. Illustrative Mathematics' Grade 6 and 7 ratio standards distinguish ratio reasoning from proportional relationships; its Grade 8 equations standard adds linear-equation work. These sources support the five-folder progression in the curriculum map.

For the first published lessons, explanations use original Arabic wording and new numbers. Each wrong answer is paired with a possible reasoning path, presented as a common source of that choice rather than a diagnosis of the student. Student-specific review is based on recorded attempts and repeated observed skill codes; no trained-machine-learning claim is made.

## Conflicts & Open Questions

ETEC guidance is the authority for exam-specific framing; open mathematics textbooks and standards are used only for the underlying mathematics. No numerical topic weighting is inferred from the textbook sequence. Assessment thresholds in the app are a product decision, not an ETEC score conversion.

## Gaps

The ETEC PDFs were located on the official media domain, but their complete text was not extracted during this pass. Exact exam blueprint and question-frequency claims remain out of scope until the current guide can be fully reviewed. Topics outside the first five lessons have source coverage and a map, but are not yet authored or published in the app.
