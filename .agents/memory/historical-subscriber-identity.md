---
name: Historical subscriber identity
description: Safe handling of subscriptions whose legacy owner no longer matches a current account.
---

Treat a legacy financial owner identity and a current student identity as distinct unless an exact, verified relationship exists. Preserve the original subscription owner reference, including legacy numeric references; do not invent students or match owners by similar names.

**Why:** Existing financial history includes legacy identities for which no current account exists. Replacing them with guessed modern identities could misattribute purchases, receipts, entitlements, or refunds.

**How to apply:** Display available verified contact data separately from the original financial reference, and explicitly mark unresolved records. Any later reconciliation needs evidence of ownership and must preserve the historical audit trail.
