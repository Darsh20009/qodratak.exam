---
name: Official score planning
description: Rules for using externally reported Qudrat scores in student planning without corrupting practice metrics.
---

External verbal and quantitative scores are a separate student input from platform practice results. They may guide the recommended focus, but must not inflate training percentages or lesson-completion metrics.

**Why:** A student’s official score measures a different experience from answered platform questions; combining them makes progress and recommendations misleading.

**How to apply:** Persist official scores with the selected program, validate each score as 0–100, and use the lower reported section to choose the next focus while leaving practice aggregates unchanged.
