---
name: Quantitative foundation attachments
description: Quantitative foundation lessons can publish with static PDF materials before their videos arrive.
---

Quantitative foundation lessons use versioned static PDF attachments inside the web artifact, while `videoUrl` remains optional until the corresponding lesson video is supplied.

**Why:** The study materials are supplied before the videos, and blocking publication on a missing video would hide useful lesson content from students.

**How to apply:** Keep each lesson's PDF link stable when adding or replacing its video; preserve the attachment metadata and expose the file from the lesson's student view.
