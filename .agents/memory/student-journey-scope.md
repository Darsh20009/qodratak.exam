---
name: Student journey and Tahsili availability
description: Product scope for reducing student confusion and withholding incomplete Tahsili features.
---

Keep one clear student preparation path personalized to observed level, exam date and available daily time. The user reported getting lost in the platform despite being its creator; more separate tools are not a substitute for clear next steps.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a connected daily course, not scattered tasks, tests and reports.

**How to apply:** Make the next task primary and supplementary tools optional. Explain weak/slow observations with evidence, keep reviewed source keys authoritative, and never promise completing the entire bank or a perfect official score when time/evidence does not support it.

Keep the student's first dashboard view short and action-first; long instructions, the guide film, feedback form and detailed coaching should be optional disclosures.

**Why:** The user explicitly said the dashboard had become too crowded with text, including after adding onboarding explanations.

**How to apply:** Preserve access to these tools and the guide's hide/restore setting, but do not force the student to read them before reaching the next task.

Keep Qudrat test types together under «الاختبارات» inside «المحوسب», including simulations, models and scheduled simulation booking.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for one section containing every Qudrat exam type rather than scattered entry points.

**How to apply:** Add future Qudrat exam entry points to this hub while retaining existing routes. A hub link is not evidence that reviewed exam content is available; state missing content clearly.

Course reading tasks must identify the specific teaching unit, not allocate a short slot to an entire book. Confirming a unit's reading must not complete the whole book or assert mastery.

**Why:** The persona audit exposed 15–22 minute assignments pointing to 150–180 minute books without a section target. Students could not tell what they were expected to finish.

**How to apply:** Keep unit reading self-reported and separate from trusted practice results and whole-content completion. Source difficulty labels and text-based lesson matches are instructional hints, not calibrated difficulty or approved concept mastery.

Tahsili is not ready for student use. Keep its student-facing lessons, tests and plans closed and labeled «قريبًا» until the user explicitly asks to reopen it after readiness work.

**Why:** The user said «المنصة التحصيلي لا يعمل بالكامل» and requested closing anything related to it.

**How to apply:** Preserve existing content, administrative maintenance and historical results; do not delete Tahsili data. Apply availability restrictions on the server as well as navigation so direct student URLs do not reopen unfinished features.

Students without a booked exam must be able to start assessment and daily learning without inventing an official exam date.

**Why:** Booking is not a prerequisite for beginning preparation; a fabricated deadline would distort urgency and the student's course.

**How to apply:** Open a daily session only when a real ready plan exists; otherwise explicitly offer published foundation material, not a fabricated personalized session or a request to wait for nonexistent new content. Intro visibility and booking-choice preferences are presentation only, never evidence of level or permission to bypass assessment/subscription checks.
