---
name: Foundation resource availability
description: Foundation pages must distinguish published books, files, and videos from missing or merely suggested material.
---

Derive the book, PDF, and video indicators from the published foundation content records associated with the selected subject. Treat loading and request failure as their own states; show «قريبًا» only after a successful response confirms that a resource is absent. Label static curriculum outlines as guidance, not published books or files.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to show only resources that are actually available and to mark missing material as «قريبًا», rather than presenting promises or empty areas.

**How to apply:** When changing the foundation entry, subject overview, or lesson player, keep resource status tied to the fetched content and avoid implying that an unlinked file or video exists.
