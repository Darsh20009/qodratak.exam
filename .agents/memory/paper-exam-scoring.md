---
name: Paper exam scoring
description: Scoring policy for digital simulations and results from paper models.
---

Count every question in new paper-model and paper-simulation results, including items historically marked as trial questions. Legacy trial metadata may remain for provenance, but must not exclude a question from a new score. Do not rewrite completed historical results when applying the policy.

**Why:** The user chose to have all questions counted after noticing 20 questions were excluded; past results should remain an accurate record of how they were originally saved.

**How to apply:** Derive result totals from each model's full question counts, ignore legacy trial flags during new grading, and keep past saved results unchanged.
