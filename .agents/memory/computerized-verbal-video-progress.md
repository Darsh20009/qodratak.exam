---
name: Computerized verbal video progress
description: Durable watch status for the fixed catalog of computerized verbal lessons.
---

Treat each fixed-catalog verbal video as its own student-owned watch record keyed by its stable catalog ID. Do not merge watch completion with quiz score, mastery, or foundation lesson completion. Completion should depend on accumulated unique, plausibly watched intervals rather than current playback position or opening the page.

**Why:** these computerized videos are not `FoundationContent` lessons, and seeking to the end must not imply that the student watched the lesson.

**How to apply:** when adding reporting or adaptive recommendations, use video-watch data as a separate evidence source and preserve student scoping.
