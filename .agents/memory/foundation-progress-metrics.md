The foundation summary currently reports performance from saved test answers, not video or lesson completion.

**Why:** The existing data records test results by program and section, but does not yet persist whether a student watched or completed a lesson. Showing that performance as lesson completion would be misleading.

**How to apply:** Keep “نسبة الأداء” or equivalent wording distinct from a future “نسبة إنجاز الدروس” metric. Add lesson completion persistence before changing the label or interpretation.
